What We Do

Four straightforward service groups.

Instead of a long list of separate pages, everything Best Choice offers is grouped here.

Weekly Lawn Maintenance

Mowing, edging, string trimming, and routine upkeep for residential and commercial properties.

Shrubs & Small Trees

Shrub pruning and trimming plus maintenance trimming for small trees under 10 feet. Best Choice does not provide tree-service work.

Turf Care

Fertilizing, weed control, aeration, dethatching, and general turf maintenance. Weed-spraying services are not currently offered.

Sprinkler Repair

Troubleshooting and repair of existing sprinkler systems. New system installation is not offered.

Full-Service Landscaping Maintenance

Recurring care without installation projects.

Best Choice can combine mowing, trimming, shrub care, turf care, edging, and sprinkler repair into an ongoing maintenance plan for your property.
